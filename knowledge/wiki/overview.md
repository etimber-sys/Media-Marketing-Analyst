# Paramount+ — Business Overview

## Company and Platform

Paramount+ is an American subscription video on-demand (SVOD) over-the-top streaming service owned by Paramount Skydance (formerly Paramount Global, formerly ViacomCBS). The service originally launched on October 28, 2014 as **CBS All Access**, created by CBS Corporation as the first OTT offering by an American broadcast television network. Following the 2019 re-merger of CBS Corporation and Viacom, the service expanded its content library to include programming from MTV, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central, BET, and Paramount Pictures. On March 4, 2021, the service rebranded as **Paramount+**, positioning itself as the flagship premium streaming venture of the combined company alongside the free tier Pluto TV.

The platform is owned by Paramount Skydance, the company formed when Skydance Media and Paramount Global received FCC approval to merge on July 24, 2025, with the merger effective August 7, 2025. CEO David Ellison has indicated a "soft merger" of Paramount+ and Pluto TV — unifying their technical infrastructure while maintaining distinct front-end identities — could be completed by late 2026. As of early 2026, Paramount Skydance is also in the process of acquiring Warner Bros. Discovery, with plans to merge Paramount+ and HBO Max into a single service targeting more than 200 million combined subscribers.

Paramount+ is one of the top ten most-subscribed SVOD services globally, available as an independent service in the US, Canada, Australia, UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Italy, Brazil, and Spanish-speaking Latin America, and through third-party distributors and the SkyShowtime joint venture across much of Europe and Asia.

The rebranding from CBS All Access to Paramount+ in March 2021 was the single largest growth catalyst, adding approximately 25 million subscribers in the first year. A Wall Street Journal report noted Paramount was planning price increases for US subscribers in early 2026.

## Pricing and Tiers

As of May 2026 (source: paramountplus-home.md):

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| Paramount+ Essential | $8.99/mo | Ad-supported; 40,000+ episodes and movies; 3 simultaneous streams |
| Paramount+ Premium | $13.99/mo | Ad-free (except live TV); full SHOWTIME content library; 4K UHD; downloads; CBS live stream |

Annual plans are available at a discount. Student pricing (50% off via SheerID) and gift cards are also offered. The Essential tier is also bundled into Walmart+ at no additional charge.

SHOWTIME was fully integrated into the Paramount+ Premium tier on June 27, 2023, replacing the earlier bundle arrangement. The Showtime Anytime standalone app was discontinued December 14, 2023, consolidating the premium streaming audience onto Paramount+. This integration added prestige TV from SHOWTIME (Yellowjackets, Dexter: Original Sin, The Curse, Cold Water) directly into the Paramount+ app.

## Content Portfolio

Paramount+ draws from a broad library of owned IP across its parent company's brands:

- **CBS Programming:** Primetime dramas, comedies, and reality (NCIS franchise, Survivor, The Late Show). Live NFL on CBS and local CBS affiliates available on the Premium plan.
- **Paramount Pictures:** Theatrical releases arrive on the platform after their theatrical window. Blockbuster titles featured include Mission: Impossible, Sonic the Hedgehog, The Running Man.
- **Taylor Sheridan Universe:** Tulsa King, Landman, 1923, Mayor of Kingstown, The Madison, Lioness — the platform's highest-profile original drama franchise.
- **Star Trek Franchise:** Multiple series including Strange New Worlds, Picard, Discovery, Lower Decks, Prodigy — the original driver of streaming subscriber growth.
- **SHOWTIME Originals:** Premium dramas and series through the Paramount+ Premium tier.
- **Nickelodeon/MTV/BET/Comedy Central:** Children's content (SpongeBob SquarePants, PAW Patrol, Avatar: The Last Airbender), reality/competition (RuPaul's Drag Race), and Black entertainment programming (The Chi, Tyler Perry's Zatima).
- **Sports:** NFL on CBS (through 2033), UEFA Champions League and Europa League (through 2030), UFC (beginning 2026, $7.7 billion 7-year deal), March Madness (NCAA basketball), The Masters and PGA Championship, Rugby World Cup (through 2029), and various international soccer rights.
- **Pluto TV:** The free AVOD (ad-supported video on demand) tier in Paramount's streaming portfolio, with 80 million monthly active users as of April 2023. Pluto operates separately from Paramount+ with a distinct free-to-consumer model.

## Strategic Priorities

- **Path to streaming profitability:** Post-pandemic phase shift from subscriber growth at any cost to profitability and content cost discipline, consistent with the broader industry trend following the end of the "streaming wars" growth period (2020–2022).
- **Skydance merger integration:** Following the August 2025 Skydance-Paramount merger, restructuring the Direct-to-Consumer division under new leadership. David Ellison (CEO) has been focused on maintaining creator relationships (e.g., Taylor Sheridan) and expanding the sports portfolio.
- **Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition:** Pending merger with WBD approved by shareholders April 23, 2026, aiming to combine Paramount+ with HBO Max to create a 200M+ subscriber platform competitive with Netflix.
- **Sports expansion:** The $7.7 billion UFC deal (2026–2033), Rugby World Cup rights, and existing NFL/UEFA/NCAA rights signal sports as a core subscriber-acquisition and retention pillar.
- **South Park deal:** $1.5 billion deal signed July 2025 to bring South Park exclusively to Paramount+ after a bidding war with Netflix and HBO Max — signals continued investment in marquee IP.
- **Pluto TV / Paramount+ soft merger:** Oracle Corporation is involved in merging the technical operations of the two platforms, with completion targeted by late 2026.
- **Price increases:** Announced plans to raise US subscription prices in early 2026.
