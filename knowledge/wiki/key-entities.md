# Paramount+ — Key Entities

## Parent Company

**Paramount Skydance** (legal name: Paramount Skydance Corporation) is the parent company of Paramount+, formed on August 7, 2025 when Skydance Media completed its merger with Paramount Global. Paramount Global itself was formerly known as ViacomCBS (2019–2022) and before that operated as two separate companies — CBS Corporation and Viacom — after the original Viacom split in 2005.

Key brands under Paramount Skydance:
- **CBS** — Broadcast television network; source of live NFL, primetime dramas, news
- **MTV** — Youth entertainment and reality programming
- **Nickelodeon** — Children's entertainment brand (in operation 45+ years)
- **BET Media Group** — Black entertainment network and programming
- **Comedy Central** — Comedy and animated programming (South Park)
- **Paramount Pictures** — Film studio with 1,000+ title library, founded 1912
- **Pluto TV** — Free ad-supported streaming television (FAST), 80M monthly active users
- **CBS Sports** — Sports broadcasting including NFL, college football, NCAA basketball, golf
- **Skydance Sports** — Joint venture with NFL and NFL Films for premium sports content
- **Skydance Animation** — High-end animated feature films and series

As of May 2026, Paramount Skydance is in the process of acquiring Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD shareholders approved the sale April 23, 2026), which would add HBO Max, Warner Bros. Pictures, and HBO to the combined company's assets.

## Content Franchises

### Taylor Sheridan Universe
The most commercially significant original franchise on Paramount+. Key titles:
- **Yellowstone** (2018–2024, Paramount Network) — 5 seasons, 53 episodes. Starring Kevin Costner. The original flagship; widely described as the most-watched cable drama in the US during its peak. Season 4 premiere drew 12.7 million viewers; season 5 premiere drew 12.1 million. Won a Golden Globe (Kevin Costner, Best Actor in a Drama Series, 2023). Note: US streaming rights were sold to Peacock (NBCUniversal) before the show's popularity surged — Paramount CEO Bob Bakish later called the deal "unfortunate."
- **1883** (2021–2022, Paramount+) — 10-episode Yellowstone prequel tracing the Dutton family's journey to Montana.
- **1923** (2022–2025, Paramount+) — 2-season Yellowstone prequel set during western expansion, featuring Harrison Ford and Helen Mirren.
- **Mayor of Kingstown** (2021–present, Paramount+) — Crime thriller starring Jeremy Renner set in a Michigan prison town.
- **Tulsa King** (2022–present, Paramount+) — Crime drama starring Sylvester Stallone, renewed through Season 4. A spinoff, **Frisco King**, starring Samuel L. Jackson, is in development (originally announced as NOLA King, relocated to Texas in February 2026).
- **Lioness** (2023–present, Paramount+) — Espionage thriller.
- **Landman** (2024–present, Paramount+) — Oil industry drama starring Billy Bob Thornton.
- **The Madison** (2026, Paramount+) — Sheridan's biggest debut season launch with 8 million global views.
- **Marshals** (2026, Paramount+) — Sheridan as executive producer.
- **Dutton Ranch** (2026, Paramount+) — Yellowstone franchise continuation, in development.

Note: Taylor Sheridan signed a five-year overall deal with NBCUniversal in October 2025, taking effect after his Paramount film deal expires March 2026 and television deal expires 2028. This represents a significant talent loss risk for Paramount+ beyond 2028.

### Star Trek Franchise
The original driver of CBS All Access subscriber growth, dating to the 2017 premiere of Star Trek: Discovery. Current and recent series on Paramount+:
- **Star Trek: Discovery** (2017–) — First Star Trek TV series since Enterprise (2001–2005); drove the largest single-day subscriber increase in CBS All Access history at launch.
- **Star Trek: Picard** (2020–2023) — Focused on The Next Generation's Captain Picard.
- **Star Trek: Lower Decks** (2020–) — Adult animated comedy.
- **Star Trek: Strange New Worlds** (Season 4 premiering July 23, 2026) — Prequel-era Enterprise crew; one of the most-anticipated current Paramount+ originals.

### SHOWTIME Originals (via Paramount+ Premium)
Following the June 2023 full integration of SHOWTIME into Paramount+ Premium:
- **Yellowjackets** — Survivor-horror drama; flagship SHOWTIME hit
- **Dexter: Original Sin** — Prequel to the Dexter franchise
- **Dexter: Resurrection** — Revival starring Michael C. Hall; Season 2 in production as of April 2026 in New York City, with Uma Thurman, Brian Cox, Neil Patrick Harris, and Eric Stonestreet joining the cast
- **The Chi** — Lena Waithe's Chicago drama; Season 8 (final season) premieres May 22, 2026
- **Yellowjackets, The Curse, Cold Water** — Additional SHOWTIME premium content

SpongeBob SquarePants, PAW Patrol, The Loud House, Fairly OddParents, Avatar: The Last Airbender, The Legend of Korra, The Patrick Star Show. Children's and family content represents a distinct subscriber segment from the adult drama audience.

## Key Figures

**Taylor Sheridan** (born May 21, 1970) — Writer, producer, director, and former actor. The single most important creative figure for Paramount+ originals. Creator of Yellowstone (co-creator with John Linson), 1883, 1923, Mayor of Kingstown, Tulsa King, Lioness, Landman, and The Madison. Wrote the screenplays for Sicario (2015), Hell or High Water (2016, Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay), and Wind River (2017). Inducted into the Texas Cowboy Hall of Fame (2021) and Texas Business Hall of Fame (2024). His departure to NBCUniversal after 2028 is a strategic challenge for Paramount+.

**David Ellison** — CEO of Paramount Skydance. Previously praised Sheridan as a "singular genius" and expressed desire to keep him at Paramount. Led the Skydance-Paramount merger and the subsequent WBD acquisition bid.

**Tom Ryan** — President of Paramount Streaming (parent of Paramount+). Also the co-founder and former CEO of Pluto TV. Reports to Cindy Holland (President, Paramount Skydance Direct-to-Consumer division) following post-merger restructuring.

**Cindy Holland** — President of Paramount Skydance Direct-to-Consumer division post-merger, overseeing both Paramount+ and Pluto TV.

## Competitor Landscape

Paramount+ competes in the SVOD market alongside (source: wikipedia-streaming-wars.md, wikipedia-paramount-plus.md):
- **Netflix** — Market leader; Paramount outbid Netflix in the WBD acquisition; Netflix also lost the South Park bidding war to Paramount
- **Disney+** — Leverages Marvel, Star Wars, Disney animated IP
- **Max (HBO Max)** — WarnerBros. Discovery's flagship; pending merger with Paramount+
- **Amazon Prime Video** — Bundled with Prime membership; serves as a distribution partner for Paramount+ in some markets
- **Peacock** — NBCUniversal; holds US streaming rights to Yellowstone seasons 1–4, creating a notable content gap on Paramount+
- **Apple TV+** — Premium original content without a large library
- **Pluto TV (free tier)** — Owned by Paramount Skydance; serves as the AVOD/free tier complementing Paramount+

Paramount+'s competitive positioning: lower price point than Netflix ($8.99 vs. Netflix's standard tier), differentiated by live sports (NFL, UFC, UEFA Champions League), the Taylor Sheridan original drama universe, Star Trek, and the SHOWTIME premium content bundle. The pending WBD merger would dramatically change the competitive landscape by combining Paramount+ with HBO Max.
