EARNINGS PRESS RELEASE | August 8, 2024
PARAMOUNT REPORTS Q2 2024 EARNINGS RESULTS

## Key Headlines
- Direct-To-Consumer Adjusted OIBDA Improved $450 Million YoY to $26 Million (first profitable quarter)
- Paramount+ Increased Revenue 46% Year-Over-Year
- Company Advances Strategic Plan, Including $500 Million in Annualized Cost Savings
- Paramount-Skydance Agreement Announced on July 7, 2024

## Q2 FINANCIAL SUMMARY

| Metric | Q2 2024 | Q2 2023 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | $6,813M | $7,616M | -11% |
| TV Media Revenue | $4,271M | $5,157M | -17% |
| Direct-to-Consumer Revenue | $1,880M | $1,665M | +13% |
| Filmed Entertainment Revenue | $679M | $831M | -18% |
| Operating Loss (GAAP) | -$5,318M | -$250M | n/m |
| Adjusted OIBDA | $867M | $606M | +43% |

Note: Q2 2024 includes a $5.98B goodwill impairment charge for the Cable Networks reporting unit, driving the GAAP operating loss.

## DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER (PARAMOUNT+)
- YTD: Paramount+ leads industry in domestic sign-ups for 4th consecutive year
- Key content drivers: Knuckles (Sonic franchise) — broke global record for most-streamed original series in premiere week; The Chi (most-streamed season in series history)
- DTC revenue increased 13% YoY
- Subscription revenue grew 12% (subscriber growth + pricing increases)
- Advertising revenue rose 16% (Paramount+ and Pluto TV growth)
- Paramount+ revenue grew 46%
- Paramount+ subscribers decreased 2.8M to 68M — reflects planned exit from hard bundle agreement in South Korea
- Paramount+ global ARPU expanded 26% YoY
- DTC Adjusted OIBDA improved $450M YoY to $26M (first profitable quarter)

## TV MEDIA
- CBS finished 2023-2024 season #1 in primetime for 16th consecutive year, including 8 of top 10 broadcast series
- Paramount and Charter Communications: new multi-year distribution agreement announced May 23, 2024
- TV Media revenue decreased 17% to $4.3B (licensing revenue fluctuations, advertising market declines)
- Adjusted OIBDA decreased 15% to $1.0B

## FILMED ENTERTAINMENT
- IF debuted #1 domestically
- A Quiet Place: Day One — franchise-best performance, $250M+ at global box office
- Revenue decreased 18% to $679M (timing of releases vs. Transformers prior year)

## SKYDANCE TRANSACTIONS
- Definitive Transaction Agreement signed July 7, 2024
- Two-step transaction: acquisition of National Amusements + new holding company formation
- 45-day go-shop period included
- Expected to close first half of 2025
