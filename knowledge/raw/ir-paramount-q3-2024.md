EARNINGS PRESS RELEASE | November 8, 2024
PARAMOUNT REPORTS Q3 2024 EARNINGS RESULTS

## Key Headlines
- Meaningful Progress Advancing Strategic Goals
- Significant Improvement in Direct-To-Consumer: Adjusted OIBDA Improved $287 Million YoY to $49 Million
- Continued Momentum at Paramount+: Revenue Growth of 25% YoY and 3.5 Million Subscriber Additions
- Streamlined Organization: Advancing $500 Million in Annual Run Rate Cost Savings

## STATEMENT FROM CO-CEOS (CHEEKS, MCCARTHY, ROBBINS)
Paramount+ added 3.5 million new subscribers in Q3, solidifying position as the #4 global SVOD service. DTC segment delivered profitability for the second quarter in a row, improving by more than $1 billion over the past four quarters.

## DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER (PARAMOUNT+)
- DTC revenue increased 10% YoY
- DTC subscription revenue grew 7% (subscriber growth + pricing increases)
- DTC advertising revenue rose 18% (Paramount+ and Pluto TV growth)
- Paramount+ revenue grew 25%
- Paramount+ subscribers increased 3.5M in quarter to 72 million total
- Paramount+ global ARPU expanded 11% YoY
- DTC Adjusted OIBDA improved $287M YoY to $49M (profitable!)
- Key drivers: return of AFL, UEFA, Tulsa King S2 (biggest global debut in platform history), Mayor of Kingstown, A Quiet Place: Day One, IF

## TV MEDIA
- CBS live news channels: strong growth in minutes viewed YoY
- The Daily Show: continued growth across streaming, linear, social
- MTV Video Music Awards: biggest audience in four years
- The Challenge: highest share in franchise history
- TV Media revenue decreased 6% to $4.3B
- TV Media Adjusted OIBDA decreased 19% to $936M

## FILMED ENTERTAINMENT
- A Quiet Place: Day One — set franchise record for biggest opening at global box office; $261M worldwide
- Transformers One — $127M at global box office
- Filmed Entertainment revenue decreased 34% to $590M
- Adjusted OIBDA improved $52M vs. prior year (which was impacted by labor strikes)

## SKYDANCE TRANSACTIONS
- Transactions anticipated to close in first half of 2025
- Paramount operating in normal course of business until closing
