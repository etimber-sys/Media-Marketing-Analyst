PARAMOUNT REPORTS Q4 AND FULL YEAR 2024 EARNINGS RESULTS

Skydance transactions expected to close in the first half of 2025

## STATEMENT FROM GEORGE CHEEKS, CHRIS MCCARTHY & BRIAN ROBBINS, CO-CEOS

We are proud of the transformative year we delivered since becoming Co-CEOs, which marks a significant turning point for Paramount as we shift into a streaming-first company. DTC profitability improved $1.2 billion in 2024, driven by an impressive year at Paramount+, where we added 10 million new subscribers and delivered a 33% increase in revenue, which gives us great confidence Paramount+ will achieve full year domestic profitability for 2025. In Q4, Paramount+ saw the highest level of engagement yet and achieved a new record, ranking as the #2 domestic SVOD service for hours watched across all Original Series.

## DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER (PARAMOUNT+)

- Q4 added 5.6 million new subscribers — best quarter of subscriber growth in two years
- Full year added 10 million subscribers, reaching 77.5 million globally
- Paramount+ ranked #2 domestic SVOD service for hours watched across all Original Series in Q4
- Paramount+ scored three of the top 10 SVOD Originals: Landman, Tulsa King, Lioness
- Domestic watch time per user reached a record high, up 22% year-over-year
- Global viewing hours increased 28% year-over-year across Paramount+ and Pluto TV
- Paramount+ revenue grew 16% in Q4; 33% for full year
- Global ARPU increased 1% year-over-year
- DTC Adjusted OIBDA improved $204M for Q4; improved ~$1.2B for full year

## TV MEDIA

- NFL on CBS delivered three of the top four regular season games, averaging 19M+ viewers on broadcast; streaming viewership up ~60% for the season
- CBS Fall 2024 primetime: seven of top 10 and 50%+ of top 30 programs, including Tracker (#1 most-watched series on television) and Matlock (#1 new series)
- Yellowstone return on Paramount Network: 18 million total viewers on premiere night, #1 show on cable for the quarter and full year
- The Daily Show: #1 cable entertainment series in Late Night
- MTV's The Challenge: #1 competition series on cable for the year
- SpongeBob: #1 Kids series

TV Media revenue declined 4% to $4.98B; affiliate/subscription revenue -7% reflecting subscriber declines.

## FILMED ENTERTAINMENT

- Sonic the Hedgehog 3: approaching ~$500M global box office (highest-grossing in franchise; franchise total $1.2B)
- Gladiator II: crossed $460M globally
- Smile 2: debuted #1 at global box office
