### Tyler Perry's Zatima — Season 4 New Episodes (04/22/2026)

NEW EPISODES OF TYLER PERRY'S ZATIMA: SEASON 4 ARRIVE ON PARAMOUNT+ MAY 12

Tyler Perry's Zatima returns with all-new episodes on Tuesday, May 12, exclusively on Paramount+. ### Dexter: Resurrection — Season 2 New Cast (04/17/2026)

BOKEEM WOODBINE AND NONA PARKER JOHNSON JOIN SEASON TWO OF DEXTER®: RESURRECTION FOR PARAMOUNT+

Bokeem Woodbine and Nona Parker Johnson will join the second season of Dexter: Resurrection in series regular roles. Johnson will play "Fiona Mixon," a training officer in the Homicide unit and Harrison's (Jack Alcott) new love interest.

### Mo Willems Children's Book Adaptations — Cast Announced (04/16/2026)

NEIL PATRICK HARRIS, TITUSS BURGESS AND ELLIE KEMPER TO STAR IN FORTHCOMING MO WILLEMS CHILDREN'S BOOK ADAPTIONS FOR PARAMOUNT+

Emmy-Nominated Unbreakable Kimmy Schmidt actors Tituss Burgess and Ellie Kemper to voice "Elephant Gerald" and "Piggie"; Four-Time Emmy Winner Neil Patrick Harris to voice "The Bus Driver" in "The Pigeon." Beloved characters created by 50-time New York Times Best-Selling Children's Author and Illustrator Mo Willems.
