## Paramount Corporate Overview (from redirected page)

Paramount is one of the world's leading producers of premium entertainment content that connects billions of people in nearly every country in the world.

## Brands Under Paramount

### Streaming
- **Paramount+** — Direct-to-consumer digital subscription video on-demand and live streaming service, combining live sports, breaking news and "A Mountain of Entertainment." Part of Paramount's global portfolio.
- **Pluto TV** — The leading free streaming television service, offering hundreds of live and linear channels and thousands of movies and television shows on demand.

### Film
- **Paramount Pictures** — A legendary producer and global distributor of filmed entertainment since 1912. Library consists of more than 1,000 film titles.

### Broadcast & Cable TV
- **CBS Entertainment** — Responsible for CBS Television Network programs, including primetime comedy, drama and reality series, specials, children's programs, daytime dramas, game and talk shows and late night programs.
- **MTV** — The world's premier youth entertainment brand, the cultural home of the millennial generation, and a pioneer in creating innovative programming for young people.
- **Nickelodeon** — In its 45th year, the number-one entertainment brand for kids. Built a diverse, global business by putting kids first.
- **Comedy Central** — Gives audiences access to funny, personally relatable, culturally relevant, provocative comedy.
- **BET Media Group** — The world's largest media company dedicated to entertaining, engaging, and empowering the Black community and championing Black culture.
- **CBS News** — Combines CBS News, 28 owned TV stations, the CBS News Streaming Network, CBS News Streaming local platforms, local websites, and cbsnews.com.

### Sports
- **CBS Sports** — Year-round leader in television sports, broadcasts college football, college basketball, golf, and more.
- **Skydance Sports** — Joint venture formed when Skydance partnered with the NFL and NFL Films to produce premium sports content.

### Animation
- **Skydance Animation** — Develops and produces high-end feature films and television series with bold, original stories set in incredible worlds.

## Post-Merger Context
The page reflects the Skydance-Paramount merger completed in 2024-2025, as evidenced by the inclusion of Skydance Sports and Skydance Animation as Paramount brands. The corporate homepage is now branded as the combined Skydance-Paramount entity.

## Key Insight for Analytics Project
Paramount+'s position within the broader Paramount portfolio shows it is the premium SVOD tier, while Pluto TV serves as the free, ad-supported tier. Together they represent a two-tier streaming strategy targeting both paying subscribers and free viewers.
