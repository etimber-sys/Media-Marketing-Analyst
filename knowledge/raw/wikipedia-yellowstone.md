_**Yellowstone**_ is an American [neo-Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_Western "Contemporary Western") [drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_(film_and_television) "Drama (film and television)") television series created by [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan "Taylor Sheridan") and [John Linson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Linson "John Linson") for [Paramount Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Network "Paramount Network"). The series premiered on June 20, 2018, and ended on December 15, 2024, after five seasons consisting of 53 episodes. It stars [Kevin Costner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Costner "Kevin Costner"), [Luke Grimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Grimes "Luke Grimes"), [Kelly Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Reilly "Kelly Reilly"), [Wes Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Bentley "Wes Bentley"), [Cole Hauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Hauser "Cole Hauser"), [Kelsey Asbille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Asbille "Kelsey Asbille"), and [Gil Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Birmingham "Gil Birmingham") in main roles. The plot follows the conflicts along the shared borders of the Yellowstone Dutton Ranch, the Broken Rock [Indian reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_reservation "Indian reservation"), [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park"), and land developers.

In 2013, Sheridan began work on the series, having recently grown tired of acting, and switched to screenwriting. Having lived in the rural parts of states such as Texas and Wyoming, Sheridan set the series in Montana and the first episodes in [Livingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livingston,_Montana "Livingston, Montana"). Sheridan initially pitched the series to [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO"), but the network declined. In May 2017, Paramount Network announced that it had [greenlit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenlit "Greenlit") its first scripted series, _Yellowstone_, and that it would be written, directed, and executive-produced by Sheridan.

The series became the first in the [_Yellowstone_ franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(franchise) "Yellowstone (franchise)"), which now includes both prequel serials _[1883](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1883_(TV_series) "1883 (TV series)")_ (2021–2022) and _[1923](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923_(TV_series) "1923 (TV series)")_ (2022–2025), _[Marshals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshals_(TV_series) "Marshals (TV series)")_ (2026), and _[Dutton Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutton_Ranch "Dutton Ranch")_ (2026), while other spin-offs remain in various stages of development.

## Premise

The series follows the Dutton family, owners of the largest cattle ranch in [Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana "Montana"), the Yellowstone Dutton Ranch (located in [Darby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darby,_Montana#Television_filmed_in_Darby "Darby, Montana")), commonly called "the Yellowstone". The plot revolves around family drama at the ranch and the bordering Broken Rock [Indian Reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Reservation "Indian Reservation"), [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park"), and land developers.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-SeriesOrder-1)

## Production

### Development

In 2013, having grown tired of acting, [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan "Taylor Sheridan") began work on the series and writing screenplays. Having lived in the rural parts of states such as Texas and Wyoming, he set the series in Montana and went about writing the first scripts in [Livingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livingston,_Montana "Livingston, Montana").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-2) Sheridan originally wrote _Yellowstone_ as a film, pitching it as " _[The Godfather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Godfather_(film) "The Godfather (film)")_ in Montana".[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-hibberd20230621-3) He initially pitched the series to [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO"), but the network declined.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Pappu-4)

In May 2017, Paramount Network announced that it had greenlit its first scripted series, _Yellowstone_. Paramount issued a series order for a first season consisting of ten episodes. The series was set to be written, directed, and executive-produced by Sheridan. Other executive producers were to include [John Linson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Linson "John Linson"), [Art Linson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Linson "Art Linson"), [Harvey Weinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Weinstein "Harvey Weinstein"), and [David C. Glasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_C._Glasser "David C. Glasser"). Production companies involved with the series were set to consist of Linson Entertainment and [The Weinstein Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weinstein_Company "The Weinstein Company").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-SeriesOrder-1)

In October 2017, it was announced that following reports of [sexual abuse allegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Weinstein_sexual_abuse_cases "Harvey Weinstein sexual abuse cases") against producer Harvey Weinstein, his name would be removed from the series' credits as would The Weinstein Company.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-5) In January 2018, Kevin Kay, president of Paramount Network, clarified during the annual [Television Critics Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_Critics_Association "Television Critics Association")'s winter press tour that _Yellowstone_ will not have The Weinstein Company's credits or logo on them, even though that company was involved in production. He stated that their intent is to replace Weinstein Television with the company's new name in the show's credits when available.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-6) That same day, it was announced that the series would premiere on June 20, 2018.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-7)

In July 2018, it was announced that Paramount Network had renewed the series for a second season that was expected to premiere in 2019.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-8) In March 2019, it was announced that the second season would premiere on June 19, 2019.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-9) In June 2019, the series was renewed by Paramount for a third season, which premiered on June 21, 2020.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-S3News-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-11) In February 2020, Paramount Network renewed the series for a fourth season, ahead of the premiere of its third season.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-12) The fourth season premiered on November 7, 2021.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-S4premieredate-13) In February 2022, Paramount Network renewed the series for a fifth season, which was split into two installments.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-14)[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-S5Renewal-15)[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-16) The fifth season premiered on November 13, 2022.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-17) In May 2023, Paramount announced that Costner would be leaving the series at the end of Part 1 of Season 5. The second part of the fifth and final season premiered on November 10, 2024.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Esquire:_Costner_Leaving-18)[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Second_Part_of_Fifth_Season_Premiere-19)

### Filming

Principal photography for the series began in August 2017 at the [Chief Joseph Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Pervais#Chief_Joseph_Ranch "Mel Pervais") near [Darby, Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darby,_Montana "Darby, Montana"), which stands in as the home of John Dutton. Filming also took place that month near [Park City, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_City,_Utah "Park City, Utah"). The production used all three soundstages at the Utah Film Studios in Park City, which is a total of 45,000 square feet. The building also houses offices, editing, a wardrobe department and construction shops. By November 2017, the series had filmed in more than twenty locations in Utah, including the Salt Flats, Promontory Club, and [Spanish Fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Fork,_Utah "Spanish Fork, Utah"). Additionally, filming also took place at various locations in Montana. Production reportedly lasted until December 2017.[\[37\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-37)[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-38)

In August 2020, the series announced that filming was completely moved to [Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana "Montana"). An undisclosed production location was rented in [Missoula, Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missoula,_Montana "Missoula, Montana").[\[39\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-39) Film locations included the Community Medical Center, Ryman Street near the County Courthouse, and a diner (Ruby's Cafe) on Brooks Street in Missoula, as well as places in nearby [Hamilton, Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_Montana "Hamilton, Montana").[\[40\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-40)

Filming for season 5 started in June 2022 in Missoula.[\[41\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-41) In May 2023, filming Season 5 Part 2 was delayed due to the [Writers Guild of America strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike "2023 Writers Guild of America strike"), due to pick back up in April 2024.

#### John Dutton's "Log Mansion"

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9e/Chief_Joseph_Ranch.jpg/500px-Chief_Joseph_Ranch.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Chief_Joseph_Ranch.jpg) Chief Joseph Ranch near Darby, Montana.

Filming of the "log mansion" home of John Dutton[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-42) is at the main house of the Chief Joseph Ranch, which is now a [guest ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guest_ranch "Guest ranch") just south of Darby.[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-43)

The house was built between 1914 and 1917 after [Cincinnati, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati,_Ohio "Cincinnati, Ohio"), residents William S. Ford (1866–1935) and [Howard Clark Hollister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Clark_Hollister "Howard Clark Hollister") (1856–1919) purchased 2,500 acres on the [Bitterroot River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitterroot_River "Bitterroot River") for a vacation home and formed the Ford-Hollister Ranch. Ford was chairman of [Owens-Illinois Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O-I_Glass "O-I Glass") in [Toledo, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio "Toledo, Ohio").[\[44\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-44) Hollister was a judge for the [United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_Ohio "United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio"). Bates & Gamble of Toledo were the architects. The house is 5,000 square feet including a 2,200 square foot parlor.[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-auto-45)

The house has a [log cabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log_cabin "Log cabin") motif and was dubbed "log mansion" by _The New York Times_.[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-auto-45) It has been compared in style to the [Old Faithful Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Faithful_Inn "Old Faithful Inn") which opened in 1904 and is 294 driving miles[\[46\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-46) away in [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park"). The _Times_ described it as "A diverse combination of arches, gables and dormers, set off by logs placed vertically and horizontally, adds an elegance to log-home design that is seldom seen."[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-auto-45)

The complex includes three large barns built to house [Holstein cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holstein_Friesian "Holstein Friesian"). It was claimed it was the largest dairy herd west of the Mississippi River. Ford later gave up the dairy cattle and began raising [Hereford cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hereford_cattle "Hereford cattle").[\[47\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-47) After Ford died, his wife and daughter operated it as a guest house. They sold it in 1952. It went through a series of new owners who renamed it for [Chief Joseph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Joseph "Chief Joseph") who is said to have passed through its area during the [Nez Perce War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nez_Perce_War "Nez Perce War"). [Mel Pervais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Pervais "Mel Pervais"), a member of the [Ojibwa Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ojibwe "Ojibwe"), owned it from 1987 to 2004.

### Music

The series' score was composed by [Brian Tyler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Tyler "Brian Tyler"). He worked with musicians from the [London Philharmonia Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Philharmonia_Orchestra "London Philharmonia Orchestra") and viola, cello, and violin soloists. On August 17, 2018, the soundtrack for the first season was released by [Sony Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music "Sony Music").[\[48\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-48)
Later on, Breton Vivian who composed additional music in season 1 & 2, has joined with Brian Tyler to score music for season three to five. [Lakeshore Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeshore_Records "Lakeshore Records") has released the season 2 through the season 5 soundtracks.

Critics have noted that _Yellowstone_'s absence from Paramount's own, similarly named streaming service, [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+ "Paramount+") (in-spite of the show's spin-offs being produced for the service), in addition to current seasons previously being available on Paramount Network's app and website primarily through [TV Everywhere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Everywhere "TV Everywhere") (requiring a paid television subscription), have caused confusion among viewers and the show's fans on [social media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media "Social media").[\[56\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-56)[\[57\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-57)

Full episodes and seasons of _Yellowstone_ are available for purchase on all major digital entertainment distribution stores in the United States, with [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company) "Amazon (company)")'s [Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Video "Prime Video") streaming new episodes in Canada the day after their American broadcast on Paramount Network.[\[58\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-58) Starting with the fifth season, it will be moved to Paramount+ in Canada,[\[59\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-59) and the UK,[\[60\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-60) and to [SkyShowtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SkyShowtime "SkyShowtime") in any territories where the service is already available.[\[61\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-61) As with the previous seasons in Canada, new episodes will be released the day after the American airing.

## Reception

### Audience viewership

Viewership of the series grew with subsequent seasons.[\[62\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Moore-62) The season 3 premiere attracted 7.6 million viewers, and the season 4 premiere tallied 12.7 million viewers.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Pappu-4) The season 5 premiere garnered 12.1 million viewers.[\[63\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Hibberd-63) The show has been called a " [Heartland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heartland_(United_States) "Heartland (United States)") drama"[\[63\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Hibberd-63) and " [red state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_states_and_blue_states "Red states and blue states")" drama,[\[64\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Poniewozik-64)[\[65\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Murray-65)[\[66\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Homonoff-66) a label which Sheridan himself disputes.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Pappu-4)

### Critical response

Following its premiere, the show was met with a mixed response from critics. [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned the first season a score of 54 out of 100 based on 27 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".[\[67\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-67) On the [review aggregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregation "Review aggregation") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the first season holds a 56% approval rating, with an average rating of 5.8/10 based on 52 reviews. The website's critical consensus of the first season reads, " _Yellowstone_ proves too melodramatic to be taken seriously, diminishing the effects of the talented cast and beautiful backdrops."[\[68\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-68)

The second season holds an approval rating of 89%, based on 9 reviews.[\[69\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-69) The third season holds an approval rating of 100% based on reviews from 7 critics.[\[70\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-70) The fourth season holds a 91% approval rating based on reviews from 11 critics. The critical consensus for that season reads, "Hitting its stride as a predictably unpredictable [oat opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oat_opera "Oat opera"), _Yellowstone_ continues to entertain with its tough-as-rawhide characters and modernized perspective on classic cowboy tropes."[\[71\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-71) The fifth season earned an 79% approval rating based on 62 reviews. The critical consensus for that season reads, "Galloping into the arena of politics with a decidedly nonpartisan bent, _Yellowstone_ enters uncertain territory but remains firmly in the saddle, with Kevin Costner's steadfast presence remaining an invaluable asset."[\[72\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-72)

### Native American representation

Native American actor [Lily Gladstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_Gladstone "Lily Gladstone") described the series' portrayal of Native Americans as "delusional" and "deplorable" in an interview with _[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)")_. She added, "No offense to the Native talent in that. I auditioned several times. That's what we had."[\[73\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-73) Associate professor of history, Liza Black, criticized the series and wrote that " _Yellowstone's_ subtext is another thing entirely: the settler-colonial version of American history, which offers didactics on human nature rather than confronting the history of Native peoples". Black adds that "Sheridan erases the history between Natives and settlers, turning Montana into a place of brute force with no national past". She was also critical of the series' depiction of Native American women in particular, noting that " _Yellowstone_ takes the historical reality and distorts it to make white women the victims."[\[74\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-74)

## Franchise

Main article: [Yellowstone (franchise)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(franchise) "Yellowstone (franchise)")

_Yellowstone_ has spawned an extensive [extended franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_franchise "Media franchise") consisting of [prequels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prequel "Prequel"), [sequels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequels "Sequels"), and [spin-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin-off_(media) "Spin-off (media)"). The first two prequels, _[1883](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1883_(TV_series) "1883 (TV series)")_ and _[1923](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923_(TV_series) "1923 (TV series)")_ take place in the titular years after which the series are named; the former explores how the Duttons originally traveled to Montana, while the latter focuses on a generation of the family during western expansion.[\[93\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-93)[\[94\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-94) _1883_ spanned ten episodes and released on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+ "Paramount+") from December 19, 2021 to February 27, 2022[\[95\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-95)[\[96\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-96)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-S4premieredate-13) It was initially announced that _1883_ itself would spin-off into another series titled _1883: The Bass Reeves Story_. This was however, later reworked into a standalone program and retitled _[Lawmen: Bass Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawmen:_Bass_Reeves "Lawmen: Bass Reeves")_.[\[97\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-97) _1923_ spanned two seasons of eight episodes each, and was also released on Paramount+ between December 18, 2022 and April 6, 2025.[\[98\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-98)[\[99\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-99) Sheridan has also been working on two further prequels set in the 1940s and 1960s.[\[100\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-100) Following the conclusion of _1923_, _1944_ was reported to be in active development.[\[101\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-101)

Following the planned departure of Costner from _Yellowstone_ a spin-off series with the working title _2024_[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Second_Part_of_Fifth_Season_Premiere-19) was announced as being in development.[\[102\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-2024Series-102)[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-Esquire:_Costner_Leaving-18) This series was later retitled _[The Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Madison_(TV_series) "The Madison (TV series)")_, however it was later confirmed as a stand-alone series no longer set in the _Yellowstone_ universe.[\[103\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-103)[\[104\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-madison-104) [Luke Grimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Grimes "Luke Grimes") as well as [Kelly Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Reilly "Kelly Reilly") and [Cole Hauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Hauser "Cole Hauser") signed on to lead separate sequels centered around their characters. Grimes' was ordered to series as _Y: Marshals_, later retitled _[Marshals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshals_(TV_series) "Marshals (TV series)")_, and is set to premiere on [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") in early 2026. It will focus on Kayce becoming a [U.S. Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Marshal "U.S. Marshal") with [Gil Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Birmingham "Gil Birmingham"), [Mo Brings Plenty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Brings_Plenty "Mo Brings Plenty"), and Brecken Merrill also reprising their roles.[\[105\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-YMarshalsOrder-105)[\[106\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-106) Reilly and Hauser are set to star in _[Dutton Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutton_Ranch "Dutton Ranch")_, along with Finn Little reprising his role.[\[107\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-107)[\[108\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-ReillyHauser-108)[\[109\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-109) Another series in development since February 2021 is _6666_, set in Texas on the [Four Sixes Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Sixes_Ranch "Four Sixes Ranch").[\[110\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_(TV_series)#cite_note-110)
